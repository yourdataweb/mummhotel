The hotel

Small, seafront and well placed

Mumm is a small hotel on the Passeig Marítim, with the beach across the promenade and the Rambla de la Pau five minutes on foot.

It is not a hotel of endless facilities: it is a large guesthouse with outside-facing rooms and personal service. What the reviews praise most is the team and the location; if you are after a pool and a spa, this is not the place.

  • The beach across the promenade
  • Outside-facing rooms with a balcony
  • Fridge and air conditioning in every room
  • The Rambla de la Pau, five minutes on foot
  • Bookings and enquiries by phone
